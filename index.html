How This Advisor Works

Why We Built This

For decades, dart players have relied on laminated poster charts or advice handed down across pub boards. While iconic, much of that traditional wisdom was invented in the 1970s and 1980s without the benefit of real data. Those charts assume you hit every treble you aim for, leaving you stranded with no plan when a dart drifts into a single.

Modern PDC match telemetry and statistical analysis have proven that many classic routes are unnecessarily risky. Today's elite game is about high-percentage recovery: taking paths that maximize your scoring surface, keep your throwing rhythm along natural vertical corridors, and leave clean double options even on a misfire.

Whether you are learning your first 2-dart checkouts or competing in league finals, this advisor replaces outdated chart habits with scientifically proven percentages—giving you the exact route and immediate recovery line for every dart in your hand.

How the Advisor Thinks

  • Track Every Dart: Options adapt instantly based on whether you have 3, 2, or 1 dart in hand. If your first dart catches a fat single, the advice immediately maps how to rescue the turn.
  • Friendly Doubles First: The engine prioritizes doubles that divide cleanly in half—like D16, Tops, D8, and D12. If you miss on the inside wire into a single, you still have an active double left without burning extra darts to adjust.
  • Smooth Arm Mechanics: Changing throwing angles across the board costs rhythm. Target paths keep your arm moving naturally down vertical channels whenever possible.
  • Your Style, Your Shot: When two routes offer equal mathematical odds (like playing for Tops versus building for D16), both are highlighted. You decide what feels best in your hand.

Route Legend

  • Best High-percentage tournament standards and co-equal baselines.
  • Alt Wire-block pivots, personal preference routes, and smart percentage plays.
  • Avoid Common match traps that leave dead-ends on single misses.
  • Setup Deliberate positioning shots when an out isn't mathematically possible.
