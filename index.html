How This Advisor Works

Why We Built This

For decades, dart players have relied on laminated poster charts or advice handed down across pub boards. While iconic, much of that traditional wisdom was invented without the benefit of real data. Those charts assume you hit every treble you aim for, leaving you stranded with no plan when a dart drifts into a single.

Modern PDC match telemetry and expected-value (EV) conversion models have proven that classic routes are unnecessarily risky. Today's elite game is built on fat-bed survivability: taking paths that guarantee a double attempt during high-friction match pressure, keeping your throwing rhythm along natural vertical corridors, and leaving clean setup options even on a 55–60% single-miss.

Whether you are learning your first 2-dart checkouts or competing in league finals, this advisor replaces outdated chart habits with mathematically optimized percentages—giving you the exact route and immediate recovery line for every dart in your hand.

How the Advisor Thinks

  • The Single-Miss Margin: Options adapt instantly based on darts in hand and match friction. If your first dart catches a fat single, the advice immediately maps the highest-percentage recovery line to rescue the turn.
  • 2ⁿ Parity Hierarchy: When opponent pressure is low, the engine prioritizes primary tier doubles that degrade cleanly on an inside wire miss (e.g., D16 → D8 → D4). This preserves setup equity without burning extra darts to adjust.
  • The Tactical Tie-Breaker: When survival math clashes with parity math, parity wins. If taking a safe single to leave the Bullseye forces you onto a dead-end odd double (like D11 or D7) on your treble attempt, the engine automatically abandons the safety net and routes you to a treble that guarantees an elite even double.
  • Kinetic Corridors: Changing angles across the board costs rhythm. Target paths keep your arm moving naturally down vertical channels (like the Top Corridor: 20, 1, 5, 12, 18) to eliminate lateral shoulder and elbow shifts within a single visit.
  • Your Style, Your Shot: When two routes offer co-equal baseline validity (like a Tops bias versus building for D16), both are highlighted. You decide what feels best in your hand.

How to Use & App Mechanics

  • Smart Keypad: To deduct a throw, simply press a multiplier followed by the number. Pressing T then 20 automatically builds the equation (`- T20`), calculates the deduction, and drops your dart count.
  • Continuous Voice Play: Tap 🎙 Voice and say a starting score (e.g., "82 with 3 darts"). Once a score is active, just speak your throw (e.g., "Trip 20" or "Bullseye"). The app automatically deducts the value and reads your next optimal route aloud.
  • Undo & Math History: The display splits to show your live math trail above your main score. If a voice command is misheard or you make a typo, tap ↶ Undo to instantly revert your score and dart count to the exact previous state.

Route Legend

  • Best Highest conversion EV based on survivability, guaranteed double equity, and parity depth.
  • Survival Gritty, high-pressure bailout routes that guarantee a dart at the Bullseye on a single miss, sacrificing ideal setup math just to stay alive.
  • Alt Valid alternative corridor or rhythm preferences, and smart percentage pivots.
  • Avoid Outdated legacy traps that strand the visit with 0% checkout probability on a single miss.
  • Setup Strategic positioning shots when a checkout is mathematically impossible or to manage bogey numbers.
